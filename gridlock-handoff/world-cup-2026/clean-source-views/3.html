    Welcome to the 2026 NXL World Cup

    Kissimmee, Florida | November 11th – 15th, 2026

    Registration Opens Tuesday, September 22nd

    Practice Registration Opens Thursday, October 8th

    Registration Closes Tuesday, November 3rd

    Layout Releases Wednesday, November 4th

    ​​​

    Get ready for the fifth and final stop of the 2026 season as the world’s premier paintball league returns to Kissimmee, Florida, for the NXL World Cup, November 11th–15th.

     

    As the season comes to a dramatic close, teams from around the globe will battle for championships, rankings, and their place in paintball history at the largest event of the year. From intense professional matchups to packed divisional competition, the World Cup brings together the very best players, brands, and fans from across the sport for one unforgettable week of action.

     

    Whether you’re competing under the Florida lights or cheering from the sidelines, the 2026 NXL World Cup is where champions are crowned and legends are made.

    ​

    Teams will compete across a variety of different NXL playing formats :

    ​

    • X-Ball : Pro, WNXL Pro, Semi-Pro & Division 2-5

    • 5v5 : Division 3, 4, 5, Women's Amateur, Masters & Youth

    • 3v3 : Amateur, Intermediate, Novice, WNXL & Youth

    • 3v3 X-Ball : Pro & Amateur

    • 10v10 : Amateur

    • Classic Mounds 5v5 : Open

    • Classic Mounds 7v7 : Open

    • Classic Mounds 10v10 : Open

    • Commanders Cup : Invitational

    • Veterans International : Invitational

    • NCPA X-Ball : College
