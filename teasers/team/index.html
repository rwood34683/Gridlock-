How to anticipate play

  1. Scout the tendency (film + past results) so you know their default break before you walk on.
  2. Read the line, pre‑break: body weighting across the box, who crowds which side, sprinter stances, where the eyes point.
  3. Read the first 2 seconds: who actually runs where confirms or kills your read — adjust instantly.
  4. Read mid‑point flow: the wire they feed is their plan; the wire they starve is your opening.
  5. Bank counters per tendency (shown above) so the whole team reacts the same way, fast.
  6. Refs: pre‑position to the anticipated hot wire, then correct on the real break.
