# How did they get there?

Open **Scout → How did they get there?**. A player's card in Scout's pits also
has this shortcut; it opens the tool with that player and their assigned bunker.

1. Choose the pit, team and player. For a team outside the list, tap **Add team**,
   enter its name and tap **Save team**. Tap **Add player** to save a name or
   nickname, jersey number, or both under that opponent. Both become selected
   immediately and remain available in the lists and in Scout's saved profiles.
   Existing team names and player numbers are reused without overwriting their
   notes. If you only need a quick number selection, use **Or enter their
   number**. You can use **Unidentified opponent** before you know the team.
2. Check the match, point and starting end. Right-pit players default to the
   right start station, moving left. Change the end when teams switch sides.
   **Start from** lets you use a known bunker instead of the start station.
3. Tap the destination on the field, or choose **Bunker they reached**. The app
   calculates possible routes around the measured bunker footprints. Where
   feasible, it offers dorito-side and snake-side alternatives.
4. To narrow the route, choose **Bunker actually seen** and tap **Record
   sighting**. Record sightings in the order you observed them. Selecting a
   destination alone does not create an observation.
5. Use **Play route**, **Previous stop** and **Next stop** to follow the route.
   The animation demonstrates movement; it does not represent measured travel
   times. **Undo last sighting** removes a mistake. **Clear this point** removes
   only the observations for the selected player, team, field, match and point.

6. **How are they shooting.** With a bunker selected above, choose **Shooting
   at** — the bunker the paint was going to — and tap **Record shot**. It is
   drawn as a white line on the field, over a black casing, the way Sightlines
   draws a lane. Recording a shot from a bunker also places him there as a
   sighting, because that is what "he's laning from the snake" means. **Undo
   last shot** removes the shot only; the sighting it placed stays unless you
   undo that too. **Clear this point** removes both.
7. **Their lanes** below counts every lane this team has been seen to shoot on
   this field, across all matches and points, most-seen first. It is a count of
   what you logged — not a prediction.

Green numbered squares show your recorded sightings. The dashed amber line
shows a possible connection between them. An unobserved destination has a
question mark. Every line between observations remains inferred: the tool has
no video tracking, GPS feed or evidence of the exact path taken.

Routes stay inside the field and avoid the supplied measured bunker footprints.
They do not assess incoming paint, quality of cover, speed or unseen players.
If no clear route can be found, the app explains that instead of drawing through
a bunker.

Sightings persist on this device and are included in **More → Nexus → Save
everything**. They are kept separate by team, player, field, match and point;
merging the same backup twice does not duplicate them. Squad-only copies do not
include match sightings. Existing breakout plans and own-player movement logs
are not relabeled as observations of an opponent.
