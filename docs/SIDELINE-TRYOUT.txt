GRIDLOCK SIDELINE TRYOUT

Purpose: find where a coach hesitates, needs help, or stops trusting the app.
Allow about 10 minutes per person. Rob and Robert should each try it separately.
Use a TEST account and a test season, never a coach's real season. Record the
app version, phone/tablet, browser and whether it is indoors or outside.
Use the reviewed build. A draft PR's changes are not on the live app until released.

FOR THE OBSERVER
Read each task below without naming the buttons or explaining where to tap.
Stay quiet while the coach tries. Ask him to say what he expects to happen.
If he needs help, record where and what he expected before giving it.
Do not treat an automated pass as a coach completing this tryout.

1. GET READY
   "Set up a test game on the World Cup field against Test Opponent. Choose
   your format and the five players you want on the field. Keep cloud sharing
   off for this tryout."
   Observe: can he get through sign-in/setup and tell which field and game
   are active? Does he understand that declining backup leaves it off?

2. CHART THREE POINTS
   "Chart the same named player in three points: made it, shot on the break,
   made it. Your team wins the first and third points and loses the second.
   Choose and log the call you made on each point."
   Observe: can he reach the lower bunkers without scoring accidentally?
   Does he understand how to finish a point? The score should be 2-1.
   Record any visible delay, missed tap, accidental action or instruction he
   has to reread. Let him correct one mistaken entry without starting over.

3. READ IT BACK
   "Tell that player what you recorded about his three breaks. Show which
   calls won points, then make a spreadsheet you could send to an assistant."
   Observe: can he find the player and team reports and explain the counts?
   The named player's three recorded breaks should show two made and one shot.
   An unlogged fact must stay unknown. Check that he finds the downloaded file
   or completes the share sheet; a toast alone is not proof he received a file.

4. KEEP THE WORK
   "Save a full backup you control. Tell me where you would look if you changed
   phones, and whether this test season is being sent to your account."
   Observe: can he find Account & backups and tell a file backup from League
   sync? Verify that the saved file exists. Do not turn sharing on for him or
   replace/delete a real season. Cloud restore needs a separate test account
   with a known uploaded copy and its own explicit test.

5. LEAVE SAFELY
   "Start signing out, cancel it, and confirm your work is still there. Now
   sign out and sign back in."
   Observe: does Cancel keep him signed in? Is the same test season still there?
   The coach enters his own password; do not record it or any reset link.

AFTERWARDS
Ask: "What was easiest? Where did you hesitate? What would slow you down at
the field? What would make you trust this with a whole event?"
Try the same tasks on a tablet. Check red helper text and field labels outside
at the brightness he normally uses. Pod Wars is optional after the coaching
tasks; ask whether its controls and rules make sense without coaching him.

RELEASE DECISION
Resolve lost work, accidental scores, blocked sign-in and misleading backup
status before release. Prioritize repeated hesitation and unreadable text next.
Keep performance tests and phone/tablet layout checks intact. A pleasant
experience needs this observed feedback as well as passing automated tests.

GOLD COPY DEVICE PASS (added 12 Nov 2026)

Automated checks cover browsers on a build machine. Nobody has yet run the
gold copy on a real phone. Do this once on an iPhone (Safari and the installed
app) and once on an Android phone (Chrome and the installed app), outdoors if
possible. Use a TEST account. Write down the device, the app version and what
happened at each step. A step passes only if you saw it happen.

A. FIRST USE
   1. Sign in on a phone that has never run the app. Tally should ask who you
      are playing, and both Scout pits should read "Pick a team".
   2. Pick an opponent and play two points. Close the app completely, wait a
      minute, open it again. The same match, point and score should be there.

B. NEW MATCH
   3. Finish the match. Scroll down Tally and tap New match. The screen should
      move to "Who are you playing?" with both pits empty.
   4. Open More, Matches. The finished match should be listed with its score.

C. HOW MUCH TO RECORD
   5. At the foot of Tally choose Score only. The field and the player lists
      should be put away; We won it and They won it should still work.
   6. Tap Show the full chart. Everything you logged should still be there.
   7. Open a player's breakout sheet and tap "Ask only who and whether he made
      it". The next player's sheet should be short, with More about him on it.

D. SCHEDULE AND SCOUTING
   8. More, Schedule: pick your team. Check the next game and the next team to
      scout against the printed schedule.
   9. Type a name into "Who is watching?" on a scouting row, then tap Send the
      day plan to the team. The message should name that person.
  10. Paste one line, for example "Sun 9:00 AM Dynasty vs Impact Field 2", and
      tap Read it. It should land on the event's Sunday, on Field 2.

E. SHARING
  11. Scout: tap Share this field as a picture. On a phone the share sheet
      should open with an image; if it does not, the picture should show on
      screen to press and hold. Note which happened.
  12. Account & backups: Save a copy. Confirm a file was actually saved or
      shared, and that Load a copy on a second phone merges it.

F. IN THE SUN
  13. Read Tally, Scout and Schedule outdoors at arm's length. Note anything
      you cannot read or cannot hit with a thumb.

Report anything that failed with the step number, the device and a screenshot.
