Support

GRIDLOCK System · Coach Edition

GRIDLOCK Coach is the sideline tool for paintball coaches and league staff. Pick the event, call the break, direct all five players, and read the other pit — between points, on a phone, with gloves on.

Get in touch

Email support@gridlockpb.com. Tell us your device and OS version, which tab you were on, and what you expected to happen. If it is a layout problem, say which event was selected.

Common questions

Where is my data? Is it backed up?
Everything lives on the phone you typed it into. There is no account, no cloud and no automatic sync — which is why the app works with no signal. Nothing backs it up for you, but you can back it up yourself: Nexus → Save a copy writes the lot to a file you can mail to yourself, drop in a note, or keep on a laptop. Do it before a tournament. If you lose the phone without one, the data is gone and we cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.
How do I move to a new phone, or share the squad with another coach?
Nexus → Save a copy, then open that file on the other phone under Load a copy. Merge keeps what is already on that phone and adds what is missing; Replace throws away what is there. Merging the same copy twice changes nothing, so it is safe to do it more than once. There is also a Just the squad copy — your players, your scouting, your bunker calls and your code words — for handing an assistant coach the useful part without your league groups and messages.
Can two coaches on the same sideline see the same data?
Not live. Each device holds its own, and nothing updates between them during a point. You can hand work across with a copy — see moving to a new phone, above — and merging is designed for it: two phones that both did real work end up with both lots.
Do I need an account?
Not to use the app, and not to join a class. A staff login is required only to create a class or send a league blast. That login is local to the device — it is not an account with us, and your password never leaves the phone.
I forgot my staff password.
There is no reset email, because there is no server to send one. Clear the app's data (Android: Settings → Apps → GRIDLOCK Coach → Storage → Clear data; iOS: delete and reinstall) and create the staff login again. This also clears your other saved work, so only do it if you have to.
How does someone join a class?
You create the session while signed in as staff; the app shows a join code. They open the app, enter the code, and give a name. No account, no email, no sign-up.
What actually happens when I send a league blast?
The app composes the message and hands it to your phone's own share sheet. You pick the app that sends it — messages, mail, a group chat. GRIDLOCK does not send anything itself and does not know where it went. The blast log on your device records that you sent it.
Why is only one event layout available?
Because every bunker on it is measured, not drawn from memory. The NXL 2026 Midwest Open is digitized from the official labeled 2D map against its printed 10-ft grid, and every bunker on the field matches that map to within a hundredth of a foot. We will not ship a layout we cannot measure — a field that is nearly right is worse than no field at all when you are calling a break off it.
The break paths — are those where my players will actually run?
No. They are a coaching model: leave from the station, reach this bunker without running through anything, here is the shortest way. They do not model the other pit's paint, who wins a lane, or the wider arc a real player takes under fire. Use them the way you would use a chalkboard.
Are the scout reads a prediction?
No. Heuristic scout reads are a training aid, not a prediction, and officials govern the live call. The rank is computed from two things you already know — how the other pit reads, and whether you can afford to be patient. It is a way to organise your own thinking, not a forecast.
Does the app work without signal?
Yes, completely. It makes no network requests. Airplane mode changes nothing.
How do I delete everything?
Uninstall the app. That removes its local storage with it. To clear it without uninstalling, use Android's Clear data, or delete and reinstall on iOS.

Reporting a problem with a layout

If a bunker looks wrong on the field, that is worth an email. Say which event, which bunker code, and what is off. Layouts are measured from official published maps, so a real mismatch is a bug we want to fix rather than a judgement call.
