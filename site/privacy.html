Privacy notice · September 11, 2026

Your season stays
on your device.

This notice describes the GRIDLOCK Coach build supplied with these pages. It has no analytics, advertising SDK, tracking service or cloud database.

What the app stores

Information you enter is saved locally: your roster, scouting observations and voice-log transcripts, matches and point sheets, field notes, lineups, player grades, messages, class details and participant responses, league groups and notices, and app settings. A local staff account stores its email and a salted password hash. The app does not send those records to a GRIDLOCK server.

Optional voice dictation

Voice log asks for microphone and, where required, speech-recognition permission only after you choose Start listening. It uses your device or browser's speech recognition provider. That provider may send audio to its servers for recognition, require an internet connection, and handle audio under its own policies. GRIDLOCK does not retain audio recordings. It saves finalized text and the associated team, player, field, match, point and time on your device; original text remains with a corrected event. Full season exports include these records. Stop listening, leaving the voice screen, changing its context or putting the app in the background ends capture. You can use typed observations without microphone permission.

Where it is kept

In a browser, the app uses that browser's local storage. An installed native build also uses the device's app storage for a second copy; that storage may be included in backups managed by your device or platform account. Clearing site data, uninstalling or resetting a device can remove local records. Device and cloud-backup providers apply their own policies.

Copies and sharing

Save a copy creates a file containing season information. You choose where to keep it and whether to send it. A share action hands information to an app or service you select; GRIDLOCK does not operate that service. Anyone with your exported season file can read its contents, including any participant or contact information in it. Keep copies with people you intend to have them.

Browser and website requests

The coaching calculations do not require a remote service. Opening the website or web app still asks its host to deliver files; the host may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address and request logs. The supplied pages do not include analytics or third-party fonts. If you follow an external store or support link, its provider handles that visit under its own policy.

Clinic participants and contacts

A coach may enter other people's names or contact details, or ask a participant to complete a local clinic sheet. Only enter information you have a reason to keep and permission to use. The person holding that device controls its records; GRIDLOCK has no remote copy to inspect or edit on their behalf.

Deleting information

Remove individual records using the app where that option is available. Undo event in Voice log removes that event's transcript and its linked route sighting from the current season. To remove browser data, use the browser's site-data controls for the address where you used GRIDLOCK. For a native installation, use the device's app-data or uninstall controls. Also delete any exports and backups you no longer want, including copies in other apps or device backups. Clearing one device does not remove copies you previously shared.

Contact

A support contact has not been configured for this distribution. Contact the person who supplied your copy of GRIDLOCK.
