Privacy Policy

GRIDLOCK System · Coach Edition  ·  Last updated 7 September 2026

The short version. GRIDLOCK Coach collects nothing about you. It has no accounts on our servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you type into the app is written to storage on your own device and stays there. We never see it, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Who this covers

This policy covers the GRIDLOCK Coach mobile app for iOS and Android (bundle id com.upra.gridlock.coach) and this website. It applies to everyone who uses the app: coaches, league staff, and anyone who joins a class with a code.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, share or store any personal information. The app makes no network requests of its own. There is no server behind it holding your data.

Because we collect nothing, there is no account to delete, no data export to request, and no third party we could have passed anything to.

What the app stores on your device

The app is a working tool, so it remembers what you put into it — entirely on the device, in the app's own local storage. That includes:

  • Your roster: player names and numbers, and their primary and secondary roles.
  • Match work: point tally, breakout calls, path and aim edits, bunker names your team uses.
  • Scout notes: what you have written about the other pit, and your own read of a matchup.
  • Classes and clinics: the sessions you create, their join codes, and the names of people who join.
  • League operations: group names, member names and phone numbers you enter, and the log of blasts you have sent.
  • Messages, walk notes, rotations and assessments you record.
  • Staff login: if you create a staff account, the app stores the email address you typed and a salted SHA-256 hash of your password — never the password itself. This login exists only to gate class creation and league blasts on this device. It is not an account with us and it is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Small preferences: which tab you were on, which event layout is selected, which tips you have dismissed.

All of this lives in the WebView's local storage and, for a few app settings, in the operating system's standard app-preferences store. Both are private to the app and to your device.

Information you enter about other people

Coaches and league staff type in other people's names and, for league groups, phone numbers. That information also never leaves the device. You are the one who decides what goes in, so you are responsible for having a good reason to record it and for keeping the device sensible about who can see it. Delete anything you no longer need — the app lets you remove roster entries, groups and members directly.

Joining a class never requires an account. A participant gives a name and a join code to the coach's device, and that response is stored there.

Saving a copy

Under Nexus → Save a copy the app can write everything you have entered into a single file — your roster, what you have logged about other teams, your tally, sessions, league groups and messages. That file is yours. Nothing sends it anywhere; the app hands it to your phone's share sheet and to your downloads, and where it goes next is entirely your choice.

Two things worth knowing before you send one to somebody:

  • A copy contains other people's information — the names you put in a roster, the names and phone numbers in your league groups, the sign-ins from a clinic, and anything you wrote about another team's players. Share it the way you would share a paper list of the same people, and only with someone who has a reason to have it.
  • A copy never contains your staff password, in any form. You sign in again on the new device.

Loading a copy back in is the same in reverse: you pick the file, and the app reads it locally. It is never uploaded.

The share sheet

Two features hand text to your phone's own share sheet: sharing a matchup card, and sending a league blast. When you tap share, the app passes the text to whichever app you pick — messages, mail, a group chat. From that moment the receiving app's privacy policy applies, not ours. The app itself does not send the message and does not know where it went.

Networking, analytics and advertising

  • No analytics. No usage data, crash reporting, session replay or event logging.
  • No advertising. No ads, no ad identifiers, no ad SDKs.
  • No tracking. We do not track you, and we do not link any data to you across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs that phone home. The app is built on Capacitor and its official plugins for app lifecycle, preferences, share sheet and status bar. None of them contacts a network.
  • The Android build declares the INTERNET permission because the WebView platform requires it. The app makes no requests with it.

The app works with no signal at all, which is the point — you use it on a sideline.

Children

GRIDLOCK Coach is a tool for coaches and league staff, not a children's app, and it is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all. A coach may write a young player's name into a roster or a class list; that stays on the coach's device under the coach's control.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Delete the app. Uninstalling removes its local storage with it. Everything is gone.
  • Clear it in place. iOS: offload or delete the app. Android: Settings → Apps → GRIDLOCK Coach → Storage → Clear data.
  • Remove individual entries — roster players, league members, groups, blast log entries — from inside the app.

There is no automatic backup and no sync. Your data exists on the device you typed it into. If you lose the phone, uninstall the app, or clear its storage, whatever was on it is gone and we cannot recover it, because we never had a copy — only you can, using Nexus → Save a copy. Keeping one before a tournament is a good habit.

Security

Data at rest is protected by your device: the operating system's app sandbox, plus whatever device encryption your passcode or biometric lock provides. Set a screen lock. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept.

This website

This site is static — plain HTML and CSS on a static host. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and contains no tracking pixels. It loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for those font files, including your IP address, the way any site that uses their fonts does. Nothing else on the page contacts a third party.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes any of the above — an optional sync or backup, for instance — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, the date at the top will change, and the app's store listing will say so. We will not quietly start collecting data.
